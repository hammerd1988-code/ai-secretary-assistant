Envoy ("we", "us") provides an AI secretary that triages messages, drafts replies,
manages calendar events, and optionally answers forwarded phone calls. This policy
explains what we collect and how we use it.

## Information we process
- **Message content you route to Envoy** (texts to your Envoy virtual number, or content
  you enter) — to classify messages, draft replies, and extract calendar events.
- **Call audio and transcripts** (only if you enable the optional Voice Receptionist) —
  to conduct the conversation, produce a summary, and capture messages/appointments.
- **Style samples you provide** — to learn your writing tone for drafting replies.
- **Settings and account data** — autonomy level, voice preferences, contacts you
  allowlist, and similar configuration.
- **Basic technical data** — logs needed to operate and secure the service.

## How we use it
- To provide the core features: triage, draft replies, schedule events, and (optionally)
  answer calls.
- To improve your experience (e.g., matching your writing style). We do **not** sell your
  personal data.

## Third-party processors
- **OpenAI** — message/call content is sent to OpenAI's API to generate classifications,
  drafts, summaries, and real-time voice responses. See OpenAI's privacy terms.
- **Twilio** — powers the optional virtual phone number for calls/texts.
- **Supabase / [hosting provider]** — stores your data if persistent storage is enabled.

## Data retention & deletion
We retain your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your data at
any time by contacting us at **[support@yourdomain.com]**.

## Your choices
- The Voice Receptionist is **off by default**; enable it only if you want call handling.
- You control autonomy levels; by default Envoy proposes actions for your approval.

## Children
Envoy is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by your jurisdiction).
