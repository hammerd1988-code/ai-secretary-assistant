# Envoy — Play Store Listing Copy (draft)

## App name
Envoy — AI Secretary

## Short description (≤80 chars)
Your AI secretary: triages texts, drafts replies in your voice, books your calendar.

## Full description
Envoy is your personal AI secretary. It reads incoming messages, understands what they
need, and drafts replies that sound like you — so you stay in control while it does the
busywork.

• Smart triage — Envoy sorts messages (scheduling, urgent, FYI, spam) so you see what
  matters first.
• Replies in your voice — it learns your tone and phrasing and drafts responses you can
  approve, edit, or send.
• Calendar automation — meeting requests become ready-to-confirm calendar events.
• Approval levels — choose how much autonomy Envoy has: suggest everything, auto-send
  after a review window, or full auto for contacts you trust.
• Optional AI voice receptionist — let a friendly AI persona answer forwarded calls,
  chat naturally with callers, take messages, and even book appointments. Turn it on only
  if you want it — the text features work fully without it.
• "I'm busy" mode — the receptionist politely explains you're tied up and captures
  anything important to pass along.

You're always in control: Envoy proposes, you approve. Nothing is sent without your
say-so unless you explicitly enable it.

## Notes for reviewers
Envoy uses a virtual phone number (via Twilio) for optional call/text handling; it does
not request device SMS or Call Log permissions. Message content is processed by OpenAI to
generate drafts and summaries; see the privacy policy for details.
